# ARIZ-85C — entry criteria and the part ladder

ARIZ is for problems that survived functional analysis and the contradiction matrix: the conflict
is tangled, the resources unclear, or the model keeps dissolving under questioning. Do not open
ARIZ for a contradiction the matrix already resolves cleanly, and do not use it to look rigorous.

## Entry criteria

Open ARIZ when any of these hold after normal modeling:

- reformulating the technical contradiction keeps shifting components or conflicts;
- the physical contradiction is known but both branches seem unavoidable on available resources;
- trimming, standards, or the matrix produced no adequate concept;
- the required change must be minimal in an already-working system.

## The nine parts, compactly

| Part | Purpose | Artifact out |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Problem analysis | State the mini-problem without special terms (step 1.1, [below](#step-11-the-mini-problem-in-plain-words)); name tool, object, and the intensified conflict | the chosen conflict model |
| 2 Problem model analysis | Define operational zone and operational time | where and when the conflict lives |
| 3 IFR and physical contradiction | State the Ideal Final Result (the X-condition) and the physical contradiction | IFR plus physical contradiction |
| 4 Substance-field resources | Mobilize everything already in the zone and time, including derivatives of resources | resource list including free ones |
| 5 Knowledge base | Apply standards, principles, separation, and effects to the physical contradiction | candidate concepts |
| 6 Change the problem | If unresolved: go to the supersystem, another system level, or the inverse problem | restated problem |
| 7 Analysis of the solution | Did the concept meet the IFR, remove the physical contradiction, introduce no new harm? | verified concept |
| 8 Utilization of the solution | Extract super-effects: where else the concept applies; how general it is | expanded concept, patentable essence |
| 9 Analysis of the process | Record which step actually solved it and what that says about the thinking | lesson for next time |

## Step 1.1: the mini-problem in plain words

ARIZ-85-В step 1.1 asks for the mini-problem to be written "без специальных терминов", in
MATRIZ's English ARIZ-85-C "without using specialized terms", on a fixed template: the system,
its purpose and main parts, the two technical contradictions, and the result required with
minimal changes ([ARIZ-85-В, Part 1](https://www.altshuller.ru/triz/ariz85v-1.asp);
[ARIZ-85-C](https://wiki.matriz.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/ARIZ-85-C.pdf)). In the step's
example the lightning rod becomes a conducting rod, or just a conductor. Note 4 limits the rule
and gives its reasons.

- **Scope.** Only the terms for the tool and the external environment are replaced. The note
  does not ask to rename the product, the element to be processed.
- **Why.** A special term:
  - imposes the old picture of how the tool works: an icebreaker is said to break ice, though
    a ship could move through ice without breaking it;
  - hides properties of the substances in the problem: formwork is not just a wall but an iron
    wall. This example is in the Russian text only; the English states the reason without it;
  - narrows the states a substance can take: "paint" suggests a liquid or a solid, though paint
    can be a gas.
- **A replacement keeps the true meaning.** The English introduction asks for non-specialised
  words that still reflect the "true" meaning. The formwork example shows that a replacement
  may add a property the term hides, not only drop detail.
- **The reminder in Part 3.** An ATTENTION box before step 3.3 asks for all ARIZ notes in
  simple, non-technical, even "childish" words, avoiding special terms, because they increase
  psychological inertia ([Part 3](https://www.altshuller.ru/triz/ariz85v-3.asp)).
- **Lineage.** The same instruction is step 2.1 in
  [ARIZ-77](https://www.altshuller.ru/triz/ariz77.asp) and in
  [ARIZ-82-Г](https://www.altshuller.ru/triz/ariz82g.asp).

In a function model, a box's `role` says which names note 4 covers: the `tool` and
`environment` boxes. The kernel's `mediator` and `supersystem` roles are not ARIZ terms;
treating their names like the tool's is this skill's reading, not the text's. The kernel's
verdicts do not depend on names, so the plain wording is for the problem statement and the
ideas; units, values, and costs stay as they are.

## Working notes

- Parts 1–4 are an interrogation of the model built in the main workflow's Diagnose and Model
  steps, not a second artifact — reuse the same frame and causal chain.
- The conflict intensification in part 1 (exaggerate the conflict to its extreme) is the cheapest
  strong move in ARIZ: it separates the parameter that actually matters from noise.
- Part 5 is where deterministic lookup earns its keep: standards, principles, and effects come
  from the tool catalog, the matrix, and the effects database — never from memory.
- Part 6 is the honest response to a dead end, not a failure: restating at the supersystem or
  inverting the problem is a standard move, and part 9 records that it was needed.

This is a map for routing, not a replacement for the text: step-level detail lives in the
MATRIZ Knowledge Base and the ARIZ-85C literature (see [sources.md](sources.md#ariz-texts),
which lists the texts the step 1.1 section was checked against).
