# Literature references

Bibliography for the categorical TRIZ theory, finite-assurance implementation, and future-work
plan. Reference identifiers are stable so design documents can cite individual entries.

The [implemented scope](../docs/categorical-assurance.md) and
[future plan](../docs/categorical-future-plan.md) distinguish adopted results, remaining proof
obligations, and research directions. A reference does not establish that the kernel meets a
paper's hypotheses or that a declared engineering model is physically valid.

## R1

## R2
